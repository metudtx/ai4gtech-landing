AI4GTech Anatolia

AI4GTech Anatolia is a European Digital Innovation Hub coordinated by METU DTX to accelerate human-centric, trustworthy AI adoption for the green and digital transformation of Türkiye’s machinery and automotive industries.

One-stop shop for industrial transformation

The hub connects companies with maturity assessment, test-before-invest services, training, access to finance, innovation ecosystem support and trustworthy AI guidance.

Service pillars

AI4GTech Anatolia structures its services around the core European Digital Innovation Hub service model, expanded with trustworthy AI and twin transformation support.

1

Test Before Invest

AI-driven prototyping, robotics, digital twins, simulation, material testing, crash testing and laboratory-based validation before major investment decisions.

2

Training and Skills

Training on AI, data governance, industrial cybersecurity, digitalisation and sustainable technology adoption for SMEs and public-sector staff.

3

Access to Finance

Advisory support to connect beneficiaries with EU and national instruments, investors, funding channels and sustainable investment roadmaps.

4

Innovation Ecosystem

Matchmaking, brokerage, collaborative R&I projects, technology provider connections and access to European AI innovation infrastructures.

5

Trustworthy AI Help Desk

First-line guidance on AI Act compliance, ethical AI deployment, GDPR alignment and referrals to AI Factories, TEFs and related EU resources.

Target sectors

The hub focuses on Türkiye’s industrial heartland, particularly machinery, automotive and related manufacturing value chains where digitalisation, AI adoption and green transition are becoming immediate competitiveness requirements.
