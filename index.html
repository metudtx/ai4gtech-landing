AI4GTech Anatolia

Coordinated by METU, AI4GTech Anatolia connects Türkiye’s machinery and automotive industries with digital maturity assessment, test-before-invest, training, access to finance and innovation ecosystem support to accelerate trustworthy AI-enabled digital and green transformation.

Strategic focus

AI-enabled twin transformation for industrial competitiveness

AI4GTech Anatolia is designed around the practical barriers companies face: readiness gaps, weak project preparation, technical validation needs, finance orientation, implementation support and evidence-backed outcome tracking.

Industrial AI

From awareness to adoption

Companies are guided from initial maturity assessment to AI use-case orientation, low-risk experimentation and adoption roadmaps tailored to operational capability.

Green transition

Digitalization as an efficiency engine

Digital and AI technologies are positioned as enablers for productivity, quality, traceability, resource efficiency, energy performance and measurable transformation outcomes.

Target sectors

Machinery and automotive

The initiative focuses on Türkiye’s machinery and automotive industries, including SMEs, mid-caps, technology providers, industrial associations and public-sector interfaces.

Transformation pathway

From assessment to implementation support

The service journey is structured to move companies from needs identification to validated solutions, skills, finance and ecosystem-enabled implementation.

Assess the need

Start with digital, AI, green and finance-readiness orientation through the METU DTX assessment logic and partner expertise.

Define the roadmap

Convert assessment findings into transformation priorities, project logic and sequencing for realistic implementation planning.

De-risk the solution

Use test-before-invest, pilots, simulation, prototyping and laboratory validation before full-scale capital deployment.

Build capability

Develop AI, data, cybersecurity, sustainable digitalisation and operational transformation capabilities through training and hands-on support.

Connect and scale

Access finance orientation, technology-provider matchmaking, European AI infrastructures and innovation ecosystem support.
